# Achievements — after the initial release

Achievements are a future goal. The first release has no achievement screen, unlock notifications, platform integration, accounts, or achievement progress saves. Gameplay does not display rescue-streak announcements.

Potential later achievements:

- A long uninterrupted rescue streak.
- A week with no leaves touching the ground.
- Restoring several grounded leaves with one Leaflift Surge.
- Completing a week without a surge, or with a specialist equipment build.
- Holding autumn through all twelve weeks and the final encounter.

Before implementing them, define precise, versioned criteria and separate them from arcade score. Use stable leaf identities and gameplay events for landings, lifting and attachment; an airborne leaf or repeated juggle must not count repeatedly. A future no-ground-contact achievement must use actual landing history, not the net frost counter, because surge recovery can remove an earlier miss from that counter.

Keep tracking quiet during play. Review achievements from an optional later menu, and allow unlock notifications to be disabled. Decide on local persistence before considering platform services; accounts/global synchronization are outside the initial release.
